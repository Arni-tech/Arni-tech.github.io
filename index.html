I grew up chasing altitude — a 12-day expedition from Manali to Ladakh, summits above 19,000 feet, the kind of terrain that teaches you to read a landscape before you trust it. That instinct carried straight into how I approach data: segment it, measure it, don't trust the summary until you've walked the ridge yourself.

That's shown up as pixel-level segmentation for coral bleaching and urban street-view analysis, dwell-time modelling across 1.3M+ check-ins for AI travel planning, and genomic prediction across 10,000+ SNP features.
